# ProDrenalin – The Professional All-in-One Optimization Solution for Action Cam and Drone Footage

## Introduction

In modern video production, footage from action cams, drones, dashcams, and mobile devices presents filmmakers with specific challenges: strong fisheye distortions, high-frequency vibrations, distracting rolling shutter distortions ("jello" effects), and insufficient contrast often reduce visual quality.

With ProDrenalin, proDAD offers a specialized complete solution that automatically and with mathematical precision transforms raw action-cam video material into professional, broadcast-ready video sequences. *ProDrenalin* combines proDAD's award-winning core technologies – including Mercalli image stabilization and DeFishr lens correction – in an intuitive application designed for speed and workflow efficiency.

* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth

## Product Versions & Evolution in Detail

### ProDrenalin v1 (2013)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Athletes, action filmmakers, and content creators looking for a fast, uncomplicated solution for optimizing action-cam material without the learning curve of complex video editors.
* **Main Features & Innovations:**
* **Automated Fisheye Rectification:** Based on the mathematical lens profiles of *DeFishr* technology. Typical barrel distortions of common action cams (e.g., GoPro) are effortlessly straightened using predefined or customizable camera profiles.
* **3D Video Stabilization:** Integration of *Mercalli* algorithms to remove tilts, jolts, and shakes along the X, Y, and Z axes.
* **CMOS & Rolling Shutter Correction:** Elimination of wobbly distortions and "jello" effects caused by line-by-line readout of CMOS sensors under vibration.
* **Color and Contrast Optimization:** Integrated tools for quick adjustment of brightness, contrast, saturation, and white balance.
* **Virtual Camera Rotation & Cropping:** Enables subsequent alignment and rotation of the horizon.


### ProDrenalin v2 (2016)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Demanding video producers, drone pilots, and action filmmakers who need a more powerful processing engine and more in-depth control options.
* **Main Features & Innovations:**
* **Improved Stabilization Engine:** Use of optimized *Mercalli v4* technology with more precise subpixel analysis and extended rolling shutter compensation.
* **More Precise Lens Correction:** Expanded profile library for modern action cams, drones, and smartphones, as well as tools for individually creating custom lens profiles.
* **Advanced Color Correction:** More detailed control of highlight, shadow, and midtone adjustments.
* **Advanced Slow-Motion and Time-Lapse Functions:** Integration of advanced interpolation methods for smooth motion sequences.
* **More Powerful Batch Processing:** Parallelized batch processing for efficient rendering of large volumes of video.


### ProDrenalin v3 (2020)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** In addition to consumers and video producers, this version is increasingly aimed at professional analysis and forensic applications, government users, and security forces.
* **Main Features & Innovations:**
* **Super Magnifier (Zoom & Detail Analysis):** High-precision digital magnification of image sections while simultaneously reconstructing fine details and edge sharpness.
* **Court-Admissible Log (Forensics Mode):** Automatic creation of tamper-proof examination logs that document every processing step, image correction, and analysis parameter completely and traceably.
* **Extended *Mercalli* Stabilization Algorithms:** Adoption of advanced *Mercalli v5* real-time and analysis engines for maximum image stability under extreme vibrations.
* **Optimized GPU Acceleration:** Drastic acceleration of computation times through native use of modern graphics processors.

## Unique Selling Points (USPs) at a Glance

1. **Mathematical Lens Unwarping via Lens Profiles:** Unlike simple optical distortion filters, *ProDrenalin* uses precisely measured physical lens models for distortion-free rectification without blurred edges.
2. **Industry-Leading *Mercalli* Stabilization:** Combination of 3D stabilization and CMOS sensor correction to eliminate high-frequency vibrations.
3. **All-in-One Workflow:** All correction steps (rectification, stabilization, color correction, editing) are performed in a single, time-saving pass.
4. **Court Admissibility from v3:** Unique, complete logging of video adjustments for government and forensic purposes.
