# Spherixr – The Professional Plug-in Solution for Spherical 360° and Panorama Video Correction

## Introduction

In the era of immersive media, virtual reality content, and spherical camera systems, video producers face a unique technical challenge: while conventional lenses and fisheye lenses capture a limited field of view (up to 180°), specialized cameras and 360° systems record the entire surroundings. The result is heavily distorted spherical or equirectangular projections that require highly specialized mathematics for conventional post-processing, re-framing, or perspective correction.

With ***Spherixr***, proDAD presents a powerful specialized plug-in that extends lens and projection correction to lenses and image formats with a field of view far beyond 180°. It seamlessly continues the classic lens dewarping (*DeFishr*) and brings proDAD's decades of experience in image processing directly to the timelines of leading professional video editing systems.

## Product Versions & Evolution in Detail

### Spherixr v6 (2026)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Professional editors and video producers of 360° and panorama content, virtual reality (VR), and specialized projections who want to adjust and re-frame highly distorted footage directly within their editing software.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* **Field-of-View Extension Beyond 180°:** Processing of spherical projections, 360° camera systems, and specialized lenses that capture more than a hemisphere (including equirectangular and binocular formats).
* **Dynamic Viewing Direction Control:** Stepless alignment of the viewing direction within 360° space via latitude (*Looking toward Latitude*) and longitude (*Looking toward Longitude*).
* **Interactive Mouse Control:** Direct and intuitive adjustment of the viewing direction using the mouse pointer in the preview window.
* **Perspective Re-Framing:** Extracting conventional flat video perspectives (standard aspect ratios) from full 360° footage, including virtual pans.
* **Mathematical Projection Control:** Precise capture of longitudes and latitudes (*Longitudes & Latitudes captured*) for accurate calculation of the spherical model.
* **Orientation & Format Correction:** Adjustment of *Display Rotation* and *Display Zoom* to correct improperly mounted cameras, as well as *Pixel Aspect Ratio Adjustment* to compensate for distorted sensor aspect ratios.
* **Schematic Analysis & Feedback Modes (*Visualization*):**
* *Globe View:* Three-dimensional sphere display showing viewing direction, framing, and image rotation.
* *North Pole View:* North pole perspective for displaying the coverage area captured by the lenses.
* *Rectangle Grid View:* Visualization of the longitude and latitude grid for precise mathematical alignment.
* **High-Performance Interpolation & Hardware Acceleration:** Interpolation methods such as *Cubic Interpolation* (for maximum sharpness and freedom from artifacts) as well as *Linear* and *Nearest Neighbor* (for fast processing modes); native GPU acceleration for smooth processing of high-resolution 4K and 8K video streams.
