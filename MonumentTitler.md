# proDAD Monument Titler: The All-in-One Titler of the Amiga Era

With the *Monument Titler*, proDAD set new standards in digital title design and video typography on the Commodore Amiga in 1994. The software combined all tools for professional titling—from the graphics editor to animation to real-time playback—on a single, well-designed user interface.

---

## *Monument Titler v1* (1994)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Exclusively for Amiga
* **Product Category:** Complete Titler / Graphics & Animation Suite

### Description & Technical Innovations
*Monument Titler v1* made it possible to precisely arrange and graphically design fonts, individual letters, and graphics directly on screen. To give static titles dynamism, the software offered extensive animation tools, including smooth fade-in and fade-out effects as well as complex motion paths.

The system was perfectly tailored to professional video and studio use:
* **Real-time control & genlock support:** Thanks to genlock support, titles and graphics could be overlaid directly live with the video signal and output.
* **Intuitive live timing:** Simple keyboard input allowed the timing of titles to be controlled precisely during operation and played back in real time.
* **Unified workspace with timeline:** Editor, animator, and playback control were combined on a single interface and structured via an integrated timeline for precise timing.
* **Integration of *Transformer* technology:** Through automatic *Transformer* conversion in the background, users no longer had to worry about different image formats or color spaces.
* **Hardware optimization:** The software optionally took full advantage of the specific graphics capabilities of Amiga hardware.

### Visual Effects & Design Options
* **Generic color gradients:** Fonts and letters could be given dynamic color gradients to create visual illusions such as metallic reflections, romantic moods, or sculpted 3D effects.
* **Highlights & lighting effects:** The targeted placement of highlights, stars, and lighting effects gave titles the typical broadcast look.

---

## *Monument Titler v2* (1995)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga
* **Product Category:** Upgrade / Titler Suite

### Description & Advancements
With version 2, proDAD expanded the titler's capabilities with advanced effects, faster render routines, and even deeper integration into existing editing workflows.

**Differences and improvements compared with the previous version (*Monument Titler v1*):**
* **Expanded effects library:** Additional light and shine particles for more dynamic title animations.
* **Optimized render engine:** Faster preview performance and refined integration of *Transformer* color quantization.
* **Improved timeline control:** Even more precise coordination of the timing of multiple layers (text, graphics, light points).

---

## Summary of Product Highlights

* **All-in-one architecture:** Editor, animator, and player on a shared interface with timeline.
* **Seamless broadcast use:** Genlock support and keyboard timing for real-time output in live video.
* **Automatic conversion:** Hassle-free format and color adjustment through integrated *Transformer* technology.
* **Creative text design:** Generic color gradients, highlights, and lighting effects for first-class visual impressions.
