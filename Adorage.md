# proDAD Adorage: The Evolution of the Effects Suite and Transition Effects

*Adorage* is one of the best-known milestones and classics in proDAD's software portfolio. Originally developed in 1991 as a groundbreaking effects library and transition software for the Amiga platform, the product successfully made the technology transition to Windows PCs in 1999. On Microsoft Windows, *Adorage* established itself as a worldwide standard for professional video transitions, dissolves, and picture-in-picture effects across a total of 13 themed Volumes.

---

## The Amiga Era: Beginnings and Pioneering Achievement (1991–1993)

In the early 1990s, *Adorage* set new quality standards in video editing and live presentations on the Commodore Amiga. Through highly optimized code and innovative rendering techniques, proDAD made complex transitions possible on systems that were severely limited in terms of hardware.

---

### *Adorage* (Amiga, 1991)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga
* **Product Category:** Effects Suite / Video Transitions

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
The original *Adorage* introduced a completely new type of image-to-image transition. While editing systems of the time usually mastered only simple hard cuts or rough linear dissolves, *Adorage* offered structured mask and particle effects.

The outstanding technical unique selling point was **SSA Animation (Super-Smooth Animation)**. This technology made it possible to play the animations with uniquely smooth, judder-free playback for the time—an achievement that no other software on the Amiga platform could offer in this form. In addition, the handling was specifically designed for direct output of the animation via a genlock: the timing could be synchronized extremely easily in real time via keyboard control. The simply designed user interface guaranteed fast and reliable use in production.

**Key Features & Unique Selling Points:**
* **SSA Animation (Super-Smooth Animation):** Uniquely smooth animation playback without judder.
* **Genlock-optimized timing:** Direct, simple control of output via keyboard control.
* **Focused ergonomics:** A simply designed user interface for fast results without a learning curve.
* Extremely efficient utilization of the Amiga graphics chipset (OCS/ECS).

---

### *Adorage v2* (Amiga, 1992)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga
* **Product Category:** Upgrade / Effects Suite

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
With version 2, proDAD expanded the engine with more precise mask controls and improved color depth. *Adorage v2* significantly optimized render times and introduced more flexible parameter settings for effects.

**Differences from the Previous Version:**
* Higher rendering speed through further optimized assembler routines.
* Expanded effects library and more flexible fine-tuning of transition duration.

---

### *Adorage AGA* (Amiga, 1993)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga
* **Product Category:** Hardware-specific Special Version

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
Adaptation to the new AGA graphics architecture (Advanced Graphics Architecture) of the Amiga models A1200 and A4000. By using up to 256 colors simultaneously, the transitions achieved a completely new visual quality without distracting color banding artifacts.

**Differences from the Previous Version:**
* Full AGA color space support (256 colors simultaneously from 16.7 million colors).
* Dramatically increased image sharpness and smoother color gradients in the transitions.
* Add-on program *Konrad* for image conversion for Adorage, which ensures image compatibility with the AGA version.

---

## The Platform Switch: From Amiga to Windows (1999)

With the change in the video market at the end of the 1990s, proDAD made the strategic decision to completely redesign Adorage's core technology for Microsoft Windows. The transition transformed Adorage from a standalone solution into a universally usable plug-in suite for almost all common NLE editing programs (non-linear editors).

---

## The Windows PC Era: *Adorage Volume Editions* (1999–2011)

On the Windows platform, *Adorage* was released in thematically structured **Volumes** (Volume 1 through Volume 13). The plug-in architecture integrated seamlessly into editors such as Adobe Premiere, MAGIX Video deluxe, Pinnacle Studio, Corel VideoStudio, or Grass Valley EDIUS.

---

### *Adorage Vol. 1* through *Adorage Vol. 3* (Windows, 1999–2000)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Windows (technology transition from Amiga)
* **Product Category:** Plug-in & Effects Suite

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
The debut on PC. The basic concept was expanded with high-resolution effects, flexible overlay techniques, and universal interface plug-ins.
* ***Adorage Vol. 1* (1999):** First Windows version. Focus on classic universal transitions, masks, and frame effects.
* ***Adorage Vol. 2* (2000):** Expanded with light, particle, and laser effects.
* ***Adorage Vol. 3* (2000):** Specialization in thematic transitions for weddings, celebrations, and family events.

---

### *Adorage Vol. 4* through *Adorage Vol. 7* (Windows, 2001–2003)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Windows
* **Product Category:** Specialized Effects Packages

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
In this generation, proDAD introduced HD-capable render engines as well as seamless integration of alpha channel information.
* ***Adorage Vol. 4* (2001):** Focus on vacation, travel, and nature footage.
* ***Adorage Vol. 5* (2001):** Focus on diamond, glass, and 3D container effects.
* ***Adorage Vol. 6* (2002):** Specialized in dynamic light rays, lens flares, and glitter effects.
* ***Adorage Vol. 7* (2003):** Focus on romance, wedding, and anniversary themes with detailed mask overlays.

---

### *Adorage Vol. 8* through *Adorage Vol. 10* (Windows, 2004–2008)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Windows
* **Product Category:** High-Definition & All-in-One Effects Packages

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
With the arrival of HDV and HD video, proDAD adapted the *Adorage* engine to higher resolutions. The software used state-of-the-art computer architectures to display thousands of effect variations in fast previews.
* ***Adorage Vol. 8* (2004):** More than 1,000 effects with a focus on sports, action, and dynamic transitions.
* ***Adorage Vol. 9* (2005):** Specialization in particle effects (fire, water, air, stars).
* ***Adorage Vol. 10* (2008):** "HD Effects Package" – First package fully optimized for HD video resolutions.

---

### *Adorage Vol. 11* through *Adorage Vol. 13* (Windows, 2009–2011)
* **Software Developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH

* **Product Category:** Premium HD Effects Libraries

**Description & Technical Innovations:**
The final generation of individual Volumes offered native 64-bit plug-in support and fully optimized HD and Full HD effects with high processing speed through GPU support.
* ***Adorage Vol. 11* (2009):** Focus on worldwide travel destinations, globe and map effects, and cultural motifs.
* ***Adorage Vol. 12* (2010):** Comprehensive universal package with over 2,000 effects for celebrations, documentaries, and video blogs.
* ***Adorage Vol. 13* (2011):** The grand finale of the Volume series ("HD All-in-One Package") with several thousand effects in a native 64-bit architecture.

## Summary of Product Highlights & Unique Selling Points

* **Milestone in software history:** Successful evolution from the Amiga pioneering era to the Windows 64-bit era.
* **Innovation leader on Amiga:** Pioneer in smooth motion sequences thanks to *SSA Animation* and genlock timing.
* **Seamless plug-in integration:** Direct availability in the timelines of all leading NLE video editing programs.
* **Resource-efficient & extremely fast:** Offers high processing speed with minimal system load thanks to optimized algorithms.
* **Huge thematic variety:** With a total of 13 Volumes, *Adorage* covers practically every visual scenario.
