# proDAD Monument Designer: The High-End Titling and Graphics System of the Amiga and NLE Era

As the "big brother" of the proven *Monument Titler*, *Monument Designer* elevated video typography and title design to a completely new, professional level starting in 1996. The software was aimed at demanding video creators, post-production studios, and broadcasters. It expanded the proven titler concept with support for true 24-bit TrueColor graphics, a full WYSIWYG editing environment with video background, and direct integration into early non-linear editing systems (NLE).

---

## *Monument Designer v1* (1996)

* **Software developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga / MacroSystem DraCo
* **Product category:** High-end titling, graphics & layout suite

### Description & Technical Innovations
While the *Monument Titler* was optimized for the classic graphics modes of the Amiga architecture, *Monument Designer* was specifically developed to fully exploit the then newly emerging TrueColor graphics cards (24-bit, 16.7 million colors).

A pioneering development step was the adaptation to MacroSystem's **DraCo** – the first Amiga clone systems without the restrictive original Amiga custom chips, specially designed as pure video workstations.

**TrueColor editor with true WYSIWYG & video background:**
* **Full WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get):** Fonts, graphics, and effects were designed directly in true 24-bit color depth without color reduction in the preview window.
* **Optional video background overlay:** Titles and vector elements could be positioned and aligned directly over an overlaid still image or a running video sequence. This eliminated blind positioning of text elements.

**Integration into MacroSystem's first NLE editing system:**
*Monument Designer v1* served as a seamlessly integrated graphics and titling module for MacroSystem's first non-linear video editing system. Titles no longer had to be prepared externally; they could be rendered and placed directly within the timeline-based video editing workflow.

**Rich creative package:**
*Monument Designer v1* included an extensive library of professional creative material:
* Numerous ready-to-use title layout templates.
* High-resolution textures, logos, graphics, and clip art.
* Color gradient sets for dimensional 3D and metal effects.

## *Monument Designer v2* (1997)

* **Software developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga / MacroSystem DraCo
* **Product category:** Major upgrade / high-end titling suite

### Description & Advancements
With version 2, proDAD further expanded the software's real-time capabilities and ergonomics. *Monument Designer v2* used the increased computing power of newer graphics and accelerator cards to process even more complex layer structures and animations without stuttering.

**Differences and improvements over the previous version (*Monument Designer v1*):**
* **Extended layer and object management:** Multi-layered layouts with multiple independent text, graphics, and light layers.
* **Extended real-time effect preview:** Even more precise adjustment of fade-ins and fade-outs, shadows, and color gradients directly in the editing interface.
* **Optimized render engine:** Significantly accelerated image computation when transferring titles to the NLE editing system.

## *Monument Designer* (Casablanca Edition, 1998)

* **Software developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** MacroSystem Casablanca
* **Product category:** Integrated standalone titling module

### Description & Special Features
In 1998, proDAD adapted *Monument Designer* to MacroSystem's revolutionary **Casablanca** platform. Casablanca was one of the world's most successful standalone hardware video editing systems (stand-alone NLE).

**Special features of the Casablanca version:**
* **Seamless system integration:** *Monument Designer* was integrated directly into the operating system and user interface of the complete Casablanca system.
* **TV-oriented operation:** Perfectly matched to control via trackball/mouse directly on the preview monitor without complex PC administration.
* **Maximum stability:** Fully optimized for the dedicated Casablanca hardware for extremely fast render times and reliability in daily editing operations.

## Summary of Product Highlights & Unique Selling Points

* **Technology pioneer:** The "big brother" of *Monument Titler* brings true 24-bit TrueColor to title design on Amiga, DraCo, and Casablanca systems.
* **Perfect WYSIWYG with video background:** Exact placement of fonts and elements directly over video footage.
* **First deep NLE integration:** Direct interface module for MacroSystem's pioneering video editing systems.
* **Extensive creative package:** Huge selection of templates, textures, clip art, and fonts included.
