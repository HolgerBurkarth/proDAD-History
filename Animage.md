# proDAD Animage: The Real-Time Compositing System of the Amiga Era

With *Animage*, proDAD released a state-of-the-art, layer-based video compositing system for the Amiga platform in 1995. The software set new standards in visual effects editing by combining a fully interactive workflow architecture with innovative particle effects and proDAD's proven SSA format (*Super-Smooth Animation*).

---

## *Animage* (Amiga, 1995)

* **Software developer:** Holger Burkarth
* **Manufacturer:** proDAD GmbH
* **Platform:** Amiga-exclusive
* **Product category:** Real-Time Video Compositing, Layer Editor & Effects System

### Description & Technical Innovations
*Animage* allowed compositing of a wide variety of media sources—such as still images, graphics, and complex video sequences—on multiple independent layers. These layers could be freely arranged, enhanced with visual effects, and controlled over time via a timeline.

**Full Interactivity & Real-Time Control:**
The standout technological feature of *Animage* was its full interactivity during operation.
* **Real-time parameter adjustment:** While the preview playback of the animation was running, the user could operate the timeline and interactively change all options or effect parameters. The visual result could be checked immediately on screen as video without disruptive render interruptions.
* **First proDAD software with non-destructive undo/redo:** *Animage* was the first product from proDAD to offer seamless and complete undo/redo functionality for absolutely all actions. This allowed extremely fast, risk-free experimentation in the creative process.

**Integrated Particle Generator:**
Another highlight was the integrated particle generator, which could be used to create impressive physical animations. Image sources or text, for example, could dynamically "shatter" into hundreds of individual pieces or be visually dissolved in space through "explosion effects."

**Export to SSA Format:**
To function seamlessly throughout the proDAD ecosystem, *Animage* offered direct conversion and output of the rendered composite into the *SSA format* (*Super-Smooth Animation*). As a result, layer compositions and particle animations created in *Animage* could be reused in other programs such as *ClariSSA* or other video tools with ultra-smooth motion and high performance.

---

## Main Features & Unique Selling Points at a Glance

* **Multi-layer compositing:** Flexible arrangement and effect layering of any image and video sources.
* **Interactive real-time workflow:** Live adjustment of all options and timeline parameters during ongoing preview playback.
* **Usability milestone:** First proDAD software with full undo/redo support for all work steps.
* **Integrated particle effects:** Convenient creation of shatter and explosion animations.
* **SSA interface:** Provides export in the proprietary SSA format for high-performance reuse in external modules and players.
