# Hide – The Advanced Tracking Solution for Automated Object Removal and Video Restoration

## Introduction

In professional video editing, post-production, and content creation for media and social networks, filmmakers often face the same challenge: distracting elements in the background ruin an otherwise perfect shot. Whether passersby who unintentionally walked into the frame, distracting vehicles, microphones, shadows, power lines, or unwanted brand logos—retouching moving video footage after the fact has historically been considered extremely time-consuming manual work (frame-by-frame retouching or rotoscoping).

With **Hide**, proDAD has created a highly specialized complete solution that removes unwanted objects from moving video images fully automatically, seamlessly, and efficiently. Originally distributed under the name **Erasr**, the application combines advanced motion analysis, precise object tracking, and intelligent AI inpainting algorithms to replace removed image elements with the logical texture of the surrounding background.

## Product Versions & Evolution in Detail

### Erasr v1 (2018)

* **Target audience & focus:** Video producers, YouTubers, filmmakers, and archivists looking for a fast, uncomplicated object removal solution without having to rely on complex rotoscoping software.
* **Key features & innovations:**
  * **Automatic object tracking:** The user roughly marks the object to be removed with a brush tool. The software then tracks the object fully automatically throughout the entire video sequence.
  * **Intelligent AI inpainting:** After the object is removed, the engine calculates the missing background from image information in preceding and subsequent frames (temporal information) as well as adjacent pixel structures.
  * **Shadow & trace correction:** In addition to the main object, its cast shadow or reflections can also be captured and removed.
  * **Integrated image stabilization:** Uses Mercalli routines to pre-stabilize the footage, which drastically increases the precision of object tracking.

### Hide v1.5 (2021)

* **Target audience & focus:** Strategic rebranding version as part of harmonizing the proDAD product portfolio (name change from *Erasr* to *Hide*).
* **Key features & innovations:**
  * **Rebranding & name change:** Official renaming of *Erasr* to *Hide* to succinctly reflect the core function—concealing and hiding unwanted image elements.
  * **Optimized processing engine:** Adoption of proven algorithms with improved system stability and optimized support for newer Windows versions and video codecs.

### Hide v2 (2022)

* **Target audience & focus:** Professional filmmakers and editors with high demands on processing speed, background synthesis, and high-resolution material (4K/8K).
* **Key features & innovations:**
  * **Improved inpainting & background synthesis:** More advanced reconstruction algorithms prevent flickering or ghosting artifacts even with complex background structures (e.g., water, meadows, patterns).
  * **More precise brush & masking tools:** Advanced tools for fine-grained masking of deforming objects (e.g., walking people or waving flags).
  * **Optimized GPU hardware acceleration:** Drastic acceleration of computation times for high-resolution 4K and 8K film material.
  * **Extended post-processing options:** Manual correction functions for difficult scenes in which the automatic tracking path must be readjusted.

## Unique Selling Propositions (USPs) at a Glance

1. **Fully automatic background synthesis:** No manual frame-by-frame retouching required; the software intelligently calculates the hidden background from the motion of the scene.
2. **Combination of object tracking and Mercalli stabilization:** Thanks to integrated pre-stabilization of the video footage, the object tracker works with extremely high accuracy.
3. **Significant time savings in post-production:** Reduces editing effort from time-intensive manual work to just a few minutes.

## Summary

**Hide** (formerly **Erasr**) is an indispensable retouching tool for modern video editing. The evolution from the first version *Erasr v1* through the rebranding step *Hide v1.5* to the highly optimized version *Hide v2* illustrates the ambition to transform complex image processing and AI tasks into simple, time-saving workflows.
