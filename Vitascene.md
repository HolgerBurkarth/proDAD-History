# Vitascene – High-End Effects, Light Filters, and Transitions in Real Time

## Introduction

In professional video editing, post-production, and broadcasting, visual effects and high-quality scene transitions often require enormous computing power. Traditional effect calculations with high-resolution source material frequently lead to long render wait times, which significantly slows down the creative workflow.

*Vitascene* from proDAD solves this challenge through the consistent use of a high-performance, fully GPU-accelerated render engine. Instead of spending time on pre-calculation, filmmakers and editors can choose from an extensive library of high-resolution transition effects, cinematic light and color filters, tilt-shift transformations, and modern seamless and glitch transitions, then apply them in true real time directly on the editing timeline, fine-tune them, and immediately evaluate them in full resolution.

## Product Versions & Evolution in Detail

### Vitascene v1 (2006)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Broadcast specialists, filmmakers, and ambitious video editors looking for real-time effects without long render times.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* Pioneering work in purely hardware-accelerated effects via graphics card (GPU rendering).
* Available as a standalone application (*Standalone*) and as a seamless plug-in for NLEs such as *Pinnacle Studio*, *MAGIX Deluxe*, *EDIUS*, and *Avid Studio*.
* Mathematically precise light ray effects (*ray effects*), glow filters, blurring, and mood-enhancing color corrections.

### Vitascene v2 (2013)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Professional editing environments requiring high system stability and 64-bit NLE integration.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* Upgrade of the engine to a fully native 64-bit architecture for increased stability and processing speed in NLEs such as *Adobe Premiere Pro* or *Sony Vegas*.
* Massive expansion of the template library to over 600 customizable filters and transitions.
* Introduction of improved miniature effects (*tilt-shift*), frame effects, and high-quality mosaic and stylization filters.

### Vitascene v3 (2017)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** High-end 4K post-production and users who need nuanced color grading and cinematic lighting accents.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* Revised, modernized user interface with optimized multi-GPU and multithreading support for smooth editing of 4K footage.
* Expansion of the catalog package to over 700 templates.
* Specialized tools for cinematic color grading, lens flares, and fine contrast lighting adjustments.

### Vitascene v4 (2020)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Content creators and video editors who demand dynamic, modern editing trends and fast navigation options.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* Integration of *seamless transitions* (seamless transitions through dynamic motion blur, rotation, zoom, and wipe effects).
* Growth of the effect lineup to over 1,400 templates.
* Implementation of an efficient tagging and search system within the user interface for rapid effect discovery.

### Vitascene v5 & v6 OFX (2022)

* **Target Audience & Focus:** Broadcasters, post-production houses, and colorists needing 8K performance, glitch aesthetics, and cross-platform OFX compatibility.
* **Key Features & Innovations:**
* Over 1,700 templates including modern trends such as *glitch effects* (digital image distortions), cyberpunk color shifts, and *light leaks*.
* Fully optimized render kernels for resolutions from SD and HD to 4K and 8K broadcast formats.
* Provision of the *Vitascene v6 OFX* interface for universal use in OpenFX platforms such as *Blackmagic DaVinci Resolve*.

## Unique Selling Points (USPs) at a Glance

1. **Consistent GPU Real-Time Performance:** Thanks to the direct computation architecture on graphics card processors, long pre-rendering times are eliminated. Even with high-resolution 4K or 8K source material, effects remain playable directly on the timeline and editable in real time.
2. **Mathematically Exact Light & Particle Calculation:** Light rays, sparks, glitter effects, and *lens flares* are calculated dynamically based on the actual brightness and color values of the underlying video image, rather than merely being stored as static graphic overlays.
3. **Flexible Hybrid Architecture:** Runs as a standalone application (*SAL*) and as a seamlessly integrable plug-in (including the OFX standard) for nearly all relevant editing systems on the market.
4. **Huge Template Variety & Scalability:** From classic light and color filters to complex *seamless* and *glitch transitions*, the suite offers over 1,700 universally customizable templates.

## Summary

Since 2006, *Vitascene* has established itself as a technological benchmark for high-performance video editing effects. Through the combination of exact mathematical light calculation, a GPU render engine continuously scaled to 8K resolutions, and universal plug-in or OFX integration, the software enables professionals and beginners alike to achieve cinematic visual results without time-consuming render interruptions.
