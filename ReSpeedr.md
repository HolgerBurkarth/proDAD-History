# ReSpeedr – The Professional High-Frame-Rate Solution for Smooth Slow Motion and Time-Lapse

## Introduction

In modern video production, slow motion and time-lapse are essential creative tools for dramatically emphasizing movement sequences, revealing details, or creating dynamic energy. The primary problem when creating slow motion after the fact from standard video footage (e.g., at 24, 30, or 60 frames per second) is the lack of image information: if the playback speed is simply reduced, it inevitably results in distracting stuttering because the editing system lacks the intermediate frames.

With ***ReSpeedr***, the German software manufacturer proDAD offers a highly advanced specialized application that computes extremely smooth slow-motion sequences and dynamic time-lapses from ordinary video recordings. By combining frame blending and state-of-the-art optical flow analysis, ***ReSpeedr*** is able to calculate mathematically precise intermediate frames and create smooth motion sequences with seemingly thousands of frames per second.

## Product Versions & Evolution in Detail

### ***ReSpeedr v1*** (2014)

* **Target audience & focus:** Sports filmmakers, action-cam users, drone pilots, and video producers who want to achieve fascinating high-speed and time-lapse effects without having to use expensive specialized cameras.
* **Main features & innovations:**
* **Dual-engine technology (optical flow & frame blending):**
* **Frame Blending:** Blends successive individual frames for fast previews or simple motion sequences.
* **Optical Flow:** Analyzes the motion vectors of each individual pixel between the original images to calculate completely new, visually seamless intermediate frames.
* **Combined slow-motion and time-lapse effects:** Creation of smooth slow-motion footage as well as precise time-lapses via keyframes within a sequence.
* **Integrated image stabilization & rolling-shutter correction:** Use of the proven ***Mercalli*** algorithms to remove camera shake and sensor distortion.
* **Intuitive keyframe control:** Row-based timeline for precise control of speed curves (speed ramping).


---

### ***ReSpeedr v2*** (2022)

* **Target audience & focus:** Professional and hobby videographers with higher demands on processing speed, modern camera formats (4K/8K, high bitrates), and accelerated production workflows.
* **Main features & innovations:**
* **Revised Optical Flow engine:** Even more precise vector analysis to avoid image artifacts (such as "warping" or "ghosting") in complex movements or overlapping objects.
* **Improved hardware acceleration (GPU support):** Drastic reduction in computing times when interpolating high-resolution video material (4K/8K).
* **Refined keyframing & UI:** Revised user interface for even more intuitive control of variable speed curves (speed ramps).
* **Expanded format support:** Native support for current video codecs and container formats.



---

## Unique Selling Points (USPs) at a Glance

1. **Hybrid optical flow calculation:** Computes high-quality intermediate frames for extremely slowed-down scenes without stuttering.
2. **Combination with ***Mercalli*** stabilization:** Prevents shakes and camera shake from being perceived more strongly due to slowdown.
3. **Seamless speed ramping:** Stepless transitions from normal speed to extreme slow motion or time-lapse within a single clip-editing sequence.
